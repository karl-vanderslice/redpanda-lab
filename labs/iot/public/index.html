      Log and retry rules

      An HTTP ingestion gateway writes iot.events.v1. A direct KafkaJS consumer rebuilds readings, receipts and alerts. This is not MQTT.

      One gateway writer, one partition. Sequence numbers must increase per device; gaps are allowed. Temperature outside the inclusive safe range opens an alert; a safe reading clears it. Battery below 20% opens an alert; 20% or more clears it. No timer clears alerts.

      Unknown completion blocks new writes. Keep the exact ID and payload, inspect its receipt, then restart the app to replay before retrying. Replay requires intact topic history.

      Synthetic sensors only. No physical devices, notifications or external providers.

# Northbridge Ledgerworks

A double-entry desk for internal transfers, fraud review and clearing.

## Design

- Navy actions and quiet neutral ledger surfaces. Tabular balances and paired entry rows make conservation visible.
- Account books, transfer decisions and review receipts remain separate. Narrow screens keep tables within their own scroll regions.
- Reserved, available and booked funds use distinct labels. Held, rejected and cleared states remain textual.

Clearing changes internal synthetic account books. No payment network or banking settlement is deployed.

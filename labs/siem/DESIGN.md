# Vesper Lantern Networks

A synthetic security correlation desk, not a verdict of compromise.

## Design

- Violet signals on neutral paper or graphite. System sans text and monospace event-time metadata keep the console legible.
- Incident queue and selected evidence share a ruled workspace; telemetry entry and the consumed timeline remain separate.
- Rule matches, rejected events and unresolved commands use text labels. A retained projection is not fresh readiness.

Analyst labels do not authenticate a user. The lab consumes synthetic normalized telemetry.

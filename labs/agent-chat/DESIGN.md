# Clockwork Correspondence

A violet-ruled conversation notebook sits between its index and schedule journal. Serif model text, quieter metadata and an in-page composer separate reading from scheduling. The journal exposes job status, due time, cancellation and log details.

Display pause leaves execution and scheduling running. An unresolved operation keeps its original ID; a missing receipt is not rejection. Due times include the local timezone and exact UTC instant in log details. Stable disclosures and controls preserve focus across updates. Theme selection works without persistence.
